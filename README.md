## 🎯 Overview & Objectives
As AI models and automated agents integrate deeply into enterprise workflows, understanding their attack surface and privacy implications is critical. This track covers critical pillars of **AI Ethics, Security Hardening, and Threat Identification**:
* **Privacy Hardening:** Securing conversational platforms and social tools against unauthorized model training and data harvesting.
* **Synthetic Media Analysis:** Identifying and classifying AI-generated deepfakes (images, audio, video) and phishing vectors.
* **Responsible AI Design:** Recognizing occupational bias, model drift, and security controls in LLMs.

## 🧪 Key Technical Labs & Modules

| Module / Lab | Objective & Focus Area | Tools / Platforms | Key Findings & Mitigations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lab 1: AI Bias Evaluation** | Assessing occupational and gender bias in generative models. | Craiyon | Identified how training data skews professional representations (e.g., nurse vs. firefighter). |
| **Lab 2: Conversational Privacy** | Hardening chat histories, telemetry, and training flags. | ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity | Disabled data-sharing toggles (e.g., *Improve the model for everyone*, *Keep activity*, *AI data retention*). |
| **Lab 3: Social Media Hardening** | Restricting data use for generative model training on networks. | LinkedIn | Turned off *Use my data for training content creation AI models* under Data Privacy settings. |
| **Lab 4: Writing Assistant Privacy** | Safeguarding drafts, emails, and notes from training harvesting. | Grammarly | Disabled *Product Improvement and Training* under Security & Privacy. |
| **Lab 5: Phishing & Scam Detection** | Analyzing fraudulent emails and URLs using multimodal AI. | Is This A Scam, ChatGPT | Evaluated visual inconsistencies, brand spoofing, and malicious phrasing vs. legitimate text. |
| **Lab 6: Deepfake Image Detection** | Authenticating images and exposing pixel-level artifacts. | DecopyAI | Distinguished real human photographs from GAN-generated synthetic faces. |
| **Lab 7: Deepfake Audio Detection** | Detecting voice cloning and synthetic speech in audio clips. | Resemble AI | Analyzed tone, frequency signatures, and robotic transitions to flag fake audio samples. |
| **Lab 8: Deepfake Video Detection** | Verifying YouTube video links for facial and lip-sync manipulation. | TrueSight | Scanned video frames and audio patterns for deepfake signatures. |

## 💡 Key Takeaways for Security Professionals

1. **Privacy by Default:** Enterprise users must actively disable conversational logging and model-training toggles across AI assistants to prevent data leakage.
2. **Multimodal Verification:** Phishing and deepfakes require robust multi-layered validation (checking metadata, audio frequency anomalies, and visual artifacts).
3. **Continuous Upskilling:** Integrating AI governance into traditional security postures (SOC, Threat Intelligence) ensures readiness against modern synthetic threats.
